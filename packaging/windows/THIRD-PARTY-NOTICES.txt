Third-party notices for the Bolo Windows CLI zip
================================================

The zip ships these prebuilt libraries next to bolo.exe, unmodified, from the
sherpa-onnx v2.0.1 release (win-x64-shared-MT-Release):
https://github.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/releases/tag/v2.0.1

bolo.exe also statically links Rust crates whose licences are allowed by the
repository's deny.toml (MIT, Apache-2.0 and similar permissive licences); the
exact versions are pinned in Cargo.lock at the release tag.
